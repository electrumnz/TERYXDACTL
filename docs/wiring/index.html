Drawing the wiring

Reading the drawing

One canvas with the whole Teryx on it. Every part is drawn once and every wire runs end to end between the two parts it joins. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to move. Grid squares are lettered down the side and numbered along the top, so C14 is a place on the canvas. Systems lights up one system's parts and wires. Earths are drawn as the earth symbol at the part, and the earth star point lists every one of them.

Opened from the simulator (Sim, then Wiring), the canvas runs live: powered wires light up, relay contacts close and lamps glow as you drive. Tap the key, a switch, the E-stop or a fuse block to work it from here.

Line colours

    Colours at the top turns these off. While the sim runs, powered wires show live instead: orange 72 V, red 12 V, amber logic, grey cut.

    Wire labels: number, size, cable colour. Cable colours are suggestions (red fused feed, black earth, orange high voltage, yellow CAN H, green CAN L) until set with colour: in wiring.yaml. Tags follow IEC 81346 letters: F fuse, K relay, Q contactor or isolator, S switch, E lamp, M motor, G source, T converter, A board or controller, X plug, B sensor, P display or speaker.

    Laid out with Eclipse ELK (elkjs, EPL-2.0). Symbols drawn to IEC 60617 shapes. Nothing here has been checked on the real Teryx yet.
