Drawing the wiring

Reading the drawing

One drawing of the whole Teryx on an unlimited grid. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to move. Grid squares are lettered down the side and numbered along the top, so C14 is a place on the drawing. Power comes first (traction, 12 V, earths), then each system. Inside a section the power circuit is on the left and the control signals on the right.

A part that has wires in more than one section is drawn in each, with only the pins used there. Blue also C14 under a part takes you to the other places. Earths are drawn as the earth symbol at the part; section 3 has the star point with every earth wire listed.

Wire labels: number, size, colour. Colours are suggestions (red fused feed, black earth, orange high voltage, yellow CAN H, green CAN L) until set with colour: in wiring.yaml. Tags follow IEC 81346 letters: F fuse, K relay, Q contactor or isolator, S switch, E lamp, M motor, G source, T converter, A board or controller, X plug, B sensor, P display or speaker.

Laid out with Eclipse ELK (elkjs, EPL-2.0). Symbols drawn to IEC 60617 shapes. Nothing here has been checked on the real Teryx yet.
